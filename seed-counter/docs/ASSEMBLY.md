# Assembly notes

Mount emitter and detector opposite one another across a matte-black chute. Shield the optical path from room light. Start with a 3–5 mm throat and test one seed size at a time; do not assume all seeds separate. Provide a removable cleanout. Verify GPIO levels before connection and keep all signals at or below 3.3 V. The detector front end as drawn is only a rough starting point; add a comparator with hysteresis before relying on counts.
