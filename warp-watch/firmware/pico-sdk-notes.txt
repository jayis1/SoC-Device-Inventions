Target integration: Raspberry Pi Pico SDK 2.0.0 with a bare RP2040 board definition.

The portable policy core is built and tested by the CMake project in this directory. A target adapter must configure: I2C0 at 400 kHz on GPIO0/GPIO1, ADS1232 DOUT GPIO2 / SCLK GPIO3, AS5600 at I2C address 0x36, OLED at 0x3C, buttons GPIO10/GPIO11, status LED GPIO6, buzzer GPIO7, and battery ADC GPIO26. Do not connect 5 V logic to RP2040 GPIO.

This repository does not include the Pico SDK or a hardware build. Verify clock, USB boot, ADC scaling, load-cell polarity, display driver, and watchdog behavior on the actual PCB before use.
