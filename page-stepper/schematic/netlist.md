# Reference wiring netlist (not KiCad capture)

- USB: Pico J1 USB connector supplies VBUS and GND.
- Pedal A: J1.1 to GP14; J1.2 to GND. Enable internal pull-up.
- Pedal B: J2.1 to GP15; J2.2 to GND. Enable internal pull-up.
- Status: onboard Pico LED GP25.
- No external supply, charger, or PCB. Use isolated dry contacts only.
