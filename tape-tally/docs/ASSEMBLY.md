# Assembly and calibration

This reference design is not a manufactured PCB. Mount the reflective sensor on a rigid, insulated bracket facing a removable high-contrast marker on the exposed supply reel. Keep all parts clear of tape and moving mechanisms. Plug the Pico into USB only; never wire the device to the deck's motor or signal circuitry. Adjust RV1 until one clean transition occurs per marker passage; confirm via USB output. Stop use if the bracket can fall into the mechanism.
